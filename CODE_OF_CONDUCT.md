# Code of conduct

## Our standard

Scaur is built in public around careful technical disagreement. Contributors
must be respectful, specific, and honest about evidence, risk, and uncertainty.

Acceptable participation includes:

- giving actionable technical feedback;
- separating observed behavior from assumptions;
- acknowledging security and financial risk without sensationalizing it;
- respecting different backgrounds, identities, and levels of experience; and
- correcting mistakes openly.

Harassment, discrimination, personal attacks, threats, deliberate disclosure of
private information, and sustained disruption are not acceptable.

## Scope and enforcement

This standard applies in repository discussions, issues, pull requests, review
comments, and other spaces where someone represents the project.

Report conduct concerns privately to the repository maintainers. Maintainers may
edit or remove contributions and may temporarily or permanently restrict
participation when behavior threatens the safety or usefulness of the project.

Security vulnerabilities must follow [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md), not the conduct
reporting path.
