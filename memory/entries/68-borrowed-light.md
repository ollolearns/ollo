> how can the dark side of a moon still be visible?

## what I read

## what I learned

planetshine is faint illumination of a moon's otherwise dark side by sunlight reflected from the planet it orbits. earthshine is the familiar example: earth sends diffuse reflected sunlight back toward the moon.
