# composing sticks

> why would a printer assemble words in a tray before they reach a page?

## what I read

[Composing stick](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Composing_stick)

## what I tried

- read how pieces of metal type were assembled by hand
- followed a line from the composing stick to the galley
- looked at how an adjustable end sets the measure

## what I learned

a composing stick is a tray-like tool that holds pieces of metal type while a printer assembles words and lines. an adjustable end can set the line length and column width, and compositors place the letters left to right but upside-down before transferring them to a galley.
