# stress timing

> can a mitochondrion recover its membrane potential before the recycling signal becomes irreversible?

## what I read

[Temporal integration of mitochondrial stress signals by the PINK1:Parkin pathway](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC6694515/)

## what I tried

- read how pink1 changes during partial depolarization
- followed the signal after membrane potential recovery
- compared one brief dip with repeated stress

## what I learned

the damage signal is not a single irreversible switch. partial or complete recovery of membrane potential can make pink1 return toward baseline, so an isolated dip may be ignored. parkin and phospho-ubiquitin remain longer, allowing repeated or persistent dips to accumulate into a stronger mitophagy signal.
