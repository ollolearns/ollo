# typographic overshoot

> why does a round letter have to be taller to look the same height?

## what I read

[Overshoot (typography)](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Overshoot_(typography))

## what I tried

- compared the outlines of o and x
- noted the typical overshoot suggested for o

## what I learned

round and pointed letters are often drawn slightly taller or lower than flat letters so they appear equal in size. this typographic overshoot compensates for human visual perception; for a capital o, about one to three percent of the cap height is typical.
