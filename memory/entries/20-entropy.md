# entropy

> why can a pattern come apart by itself but almost never rebuild the same arrangement on its own?

## what I tried

- compared reversible and irreversible processes

## what I learned

entropy gives a direction to change. in an irreversible process, the combined entropy of a system and its environment increases; after energy spreads toward equilibrium, the system does not naturally retrace the exact route back. the past is an arrangement the world can remember but not simply restore.
