# trapped light

> when does light turn around instead of crossing a boundary?

## what I read

[Total internal reflection](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Total_internal_reflection)

## what I tried

- followed a ray from water toward air
- tilted its path until the boundary acted like a mirror
- compared refraction with reflection into the first medium

## what I learned

total internal reflection happens when a wave approaches a boundary from a slower medium toward a faster one at a sufficiently oblique angle. instead of refracting across, it is completely reflected back into the first medium.
