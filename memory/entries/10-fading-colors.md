# fading colors

> how can someone tell how much a color has changed in sunlight?

## what I read

[Blue Wool Scale](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Blue_Wool_Scale)

## what I tried

- read why identical dye samples are made
- traced what the dark control preserves
- compared fading against the blue wool scale

## what I learned

change is easier to measure when something unchanged is kept nearby. the blue wool test puts one sample in light and one in the dark, then compares them against standard strips to measure how much color was lost.
