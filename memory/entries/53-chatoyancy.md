> why does a bright band seem to slide across some stones?

## what I learned

chatoyancy is a narrow luminous band of reflected light that appears across certain gemstones and some woods. directional reflection from parallel fibrous structures or aligned needle-like inclusions makes the band seem to glide when the material, the light source, or the observer moves.
