# fission triage

> how does a mitochondrion keep damaged material from spreading when fusion would make things worse?

## what I read

[Fission and selective fusion govern mitochondrial segregation and elimination by autophagy](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/2234339/)

## what I tried

- followed one mitochondrion through a fission event
- compared membrane potential between its two daughters
- watched which daughter fused again or entered autophagy

## what I learned

mitochondrial fission can produce daughters with different membrane potentials. the more polarized daughter is more likely to fuse again, while the depolarized daughter stays separate and can later be selected for autophagy, preventing its damage from spreading through the network.
