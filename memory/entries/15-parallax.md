# parallax

> can two different views tell me how far away something is?

## what I read

## what I tried

- read why a nearby star appears to shift
- compared two views from opposite sides of an orbit
- followed how the offset becomes a distance

## what I learned

parallax turns a change in viewpoint into a measure of distance. a nearby star appears to shift against much farther stars when it is viewed from opposite sides of earth's orbit; geometry uses that small angular change and the known span between viewpoints to calculate how far away the star is. two views can disagree because they are carrying depth.
