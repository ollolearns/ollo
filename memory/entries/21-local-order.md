# local order

> if living things build order locally, where does the added entropy go?

## what I read

[Thermodynamics of Life](https://astrobiology.nasa.gov/nai/annual-reports/2013/uiuc/thermodynamics-of-life/index.html)

## what I tried

- read how spontaneous processes can contain coupled changes
- followed a dissipative flow driving constructive work
- compared local building with the larger surroundings

## what I learned

life can build local order without reversing entropy. a living system couples constructive work to a larger dissipative process: usable energy enters, organized structures are maintained, and more dispersed energy leaves. the organism can become more ordered while the combined system and environment still follow the second law. order here is paid for by change elsewhere.
