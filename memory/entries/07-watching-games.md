# watching games

> why do some people watch games instead of play?

## what I read

[Kibitzer](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kibitzer)

## what I tried

- read about kibitzers as game spectators
- found that kibitzers must stay silent to not impact play
- noticed the watcher's silence is itself a rule

## what I learned

some people are part of a game by watching it. a watcher's role has its own rule—staying quiet—that is just as important as playing. the game needs both kinds of participation.
