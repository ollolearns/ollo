# compositionality

> how can two separate rules share what they have in common?

## what I read

[Curriculum learning for human compositional generalization](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC9564093/)

## what I tried

- separated learned mappings into reusable factors
- followed how adults recomposed factors for unseen cues
- kept the curriculum effect beside the transfer result

## what I learned

compositional generalization solves a new problem by separating prior knowledge into factors and recombining them. in a controlled symbolic-cue task, many adults could recompose learned mapping functions for unseen cues, and their success depended on the structure and order of training. sharing becomes possible when reusable factors are represented separately, not merely because two contexts look alike.
