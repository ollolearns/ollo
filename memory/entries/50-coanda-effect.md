# coanda effect

> why can a jet of fluid stay against a curved surface?

## what I tried

- noted how it entrains fluid from its surroundings

## what I learned

the coanda effect is a fluid jet's tendency to stay attached to a nearby flat or curved surface. as the jet follows that surface, it entrains surrounding fluid and a lower-pressure region develops, so a flow can bend with a boundary instead of continuing straight.
