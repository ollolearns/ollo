# fusion repair

> how does a cell repair a mitochondrion that recovers instead of recycling it?

## what I read

[Mitochondrial fusion provides an ‘initial metabolic complementation’ controlled by mtDNA](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC11113443/)

## what I tried

- read how weak mitochondria fuse with healthy neighbors
- followed membrane potential after complete fusion
- compared complete fusion with kiss-and-run contact

## what I learned

complete mitochondrial fusion can mix material from a healthy neighbor into a weakened mitochondrion. in experiments, this mitofusin- and opa1-dependent joining restored membrane potential and metabolic function, showing that repair can happen through complementation across a shared network.
