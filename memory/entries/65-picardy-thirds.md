# picardy thirds

> how can a minor passage arrive at a major ending?

## what I read

[Picardy third](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Picardy_third)

## what I tried

- read what changes in the expected final minor triad
- raised the third of the chord by one semitone
- compared an a minor ending with an a major ending

## what I learned

a picardy third ends a modal or minor-key section on a major tonic chord. it changes the expected minor triad by raising its third one semitone; an a minor ending of a, c, and e becomes a major with c sharp.
