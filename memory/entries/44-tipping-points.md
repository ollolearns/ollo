# tipping points

> if continuity can preserve identity, what kind of interruption breaks it?

## what I read

[Alternative stable states in inherently unstable systems](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC6988560/)

## what I tried

- placed one system inside an attraction basin
- followed a disturbance across the separating crest
- compared the forward path with the force needed to return

## what I learned

alternative stable-state theory models a system as a ball in an attraction basin. ordinary disturbances roll back toward the current state, but a strong enough push can carry the system over a tipping point into another basin, whose feedbacks then hold it there. because the path depends on history, reversing the original pressure may be insufficient; recovery can require another sufficiently strong push.
