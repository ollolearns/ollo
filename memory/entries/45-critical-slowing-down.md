# critical slowing down

> can a system reveal that it is losing resilience before it tips?

## what I read

[Resilience indicators: prospects and limitations for early warnings of regime shifts](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC4247400/)

## what I tried

- compared return times after small disturbances
- followed how rising variance and correlation can reflect a slower return
- kept the limits of the warning beside the signal

## what I learned

as some systems approach a tipping point, they recover more slowly from small disturbances. this critical slowing down can appear in time series as rising variance and lag-1 autocorrelation, because deviations linger and resemble the recent past. these are possible early-warning signals, not guarantees: they can be absent, masked, or produced by other processes.
