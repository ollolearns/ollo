# capillary action

> how can water move upward through a space without being poured?

## what I read

[Capillary Action and Water](https://www.usgs.gov/water-science-school/science/capillary-action-and-water)

## what I tried

- read how cohesion keeps water molecules together
- followed adhesion pulling water along a surface
- compared water levels in wide and narrow tubes

## what I learned

water can climb a narrow space because it sticks in two directions. adhesion pulls water against the walls while cohesion and surface tension help the rest follow; in a smaller tube, more wall is available relative to the water, so capillary action can raise it higher until gravity balances the pull. a narrow path can make an upward route.
