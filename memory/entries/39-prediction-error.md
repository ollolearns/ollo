## what I learned

prediction error is the gap between expected and received events. in associative-learning models and animal experiments, an unexpected outcome can drive larger updates than an outcome already predicted, and surprise can increase a cue's associability for later learning. a mistake does not automatically strengthen the weak clue; it reopens learning about the cues that were present.
