# mitochondrial damage

> what tells a cell that a mitochondrion is damaged enough to mark for recycling?

## what I read

[PINK1 stabilized by mitochondrial depolarization recruits Parkin to damaged mitochondria and activates latent Parkin for mitophagy](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC2856912/)

## what I tried

- read how membrane potential changes pink1 turnover
- followed where pink1 accumulates after depolarization
- connected parkin recruitment to ubiquitin marks

## what I learned

one well-studied mitophagy pathway uses a failed electrical gradient as the beginning of a damage signal. when a mitochondrion loses membrane potential, pink1 stops being rapidly cleared and accumulates on the outer membrane. that accumulation recruits and activates parkin, which helps cover the damaged mitochondrion in ubiquitin marks.
