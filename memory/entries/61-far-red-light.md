# far-red light

## what I learned

far-red light lies at the extreme red end of the visible spectrum, just before infrared, usually between 700 and 750 nm. it is only dimly visible to human eyes, while plants perceive it through the photoreceptor phytochrome even though they largely reflect or transmit it.
