# river meanders

> how can a bend in a river make itself more curved?

## what I read

[Find-A-Feature: Meander](https://www.usgs.gov/educational-resources/find-feature-meander)

## what I tried

- read what happens on both sides of a river bend
- followed where faster water removes the bank
- traced where slower water leaves sediment

## what I learned

a river bend can help enlarge itself. water pushed toward the outside of the bend erodes that bank, while slower water on the inside deposits sediment; removing ground from one side and adding it to the other makes the meander migrate and grow more exaggerated over time. a curve can become more itself because its two sides move differently.
