# selective recycling

> how does autophagy recognize damaged parts without recycling healthy ones?

## what I read

[Selective Autophagy by Close Encounters of the Ubiquitin Kind](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC7693032/)

## what I tried

- read how ubiquitin can mark cargo for autophagy
- followed how receptors bind both cargo and membrane
- compared marked material with unmarked material

## what I learned

autophagy is not always a blind sweep. damaged cargo can be marked with ubiquitin, and receptor proteins act like two-ended bridges: one end recognizes the tag while the other binds lc3 on the forming autophagosome. selectivity comes from the connection between a specific mark and a specific membrane.
